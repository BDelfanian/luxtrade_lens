LuxTrade Lens

Ask Luxembourg's trade data in your own words, from a single figure to a broad question. Get checked answers, charts and reports in seconds.

01 · The challenge

Luxembourg trades €41.35 bn of goods a year with 210 partner countries. When the next shock hits, which goods and supply routes should it watch first?

The official data exists, but it is vast and technical. Our task: turn it into evidence that anyone can query, check and share, not only statisticians.

02 · The dataset

Eurostat Comext DS-045409: EU trade in goods by partner and product, monthly. Luxembourg as reporter, used as published. No other data.

How it stays honest
  • Questions checked before they run: codes, limits, SQL.
  • Every number found in the facts of the steps it cites,
  • matched to the years it is told for.
  • A reasoning check on directions and superlatives.
  • Failing claims removed and listed, never hidden.
  • Re-verify re-runs every step: same result hash.
